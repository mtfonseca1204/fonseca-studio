Problem

Institutions had to audit Market Maker performance, but checked everything with scattered, expensive tools: cross-checking cost a lot and ate up hours. Worse, when the numbers didn’t add up the company itself became the victim: too hard to prove the breach to apply the penalty. When a penalty decision is on the line, partial data isn’t enough.

Study

Worked with operators on a single-screen model: binary SLA, liquidity over time, sidebar + filters, and progressive drill-down.

Result

Task completion rose from 61% to 88%. Checks dropped from ~20 minutes to under 30 seconds, validated in production with Kraken ~6M funded accounts and Worldchain 24M+ World App users: personalized contracts and multiple pools monitored 24/7.
