Who is Matheus Fonseca?

I am a senior product designer based in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. I work at the intersection of product, brand, technology, and AI. I have roots in art direction and more than five years building digital products. Brand: Fonseca Studio.

What kind of roles and work fit?

Product design for complex digital products: zero-to-one, UX/UI, design systems, brand and visual identity, discovery through hand-off. I am especially strong when a dense domain (Web3/DeFi, B2B tools, healthcare) needs to become something people can actually use.

Where are you based, and are you available?

Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. Available for remote work worldwide. Open to select projects. Best first step: email fonsecaa.design@gmail.com.

What is the selected work?

  • Hedgehog prediction market product: pooled UP/DOWN rounds instead of an orderbook.
  • Hedgehog waitlist & landing: institutional credibility before the product existed.

  • Unimed Seguros: trust-first telemedicine for insurance beneficiaries.

What results can you cite?
